# Optional ComfyUI shots

The briefing picture is drawn so the labels stay exact: sha-256, SHACL, passage identifiers, and the decline sentence the product actually shows. This environment has no ComfyUI endpoint, and the image-and-video generation repository is not in the workspace, so these shots were not rendered.

They are atmosphere for under the same voice, if you want a second layer from the local or Comfy Cloud pipeline (`prompts-to-scene`, or an LTX text-to-video run). Do not ask the model to draw words, numbers, screens, or a user interface. The motion-graphics frames already carry the terms. Spend Comfy Cloud credits only when you mean to.

Each shot is one still prompt and one motion prompt. Five seconds, 16:9, no people, no readable text.

## 1. Under “the model writes the answer” (section 1)

Still: A dark navy room, a few loose paper sheets on a black table, a thin gold edge-light, fine grain, no letters, no logos, no screens.

Motion: The sheets lift and separate, then settle into a loose pile. Slow push in. No new objects.

## 2. Under bronze (section 3, the hash)

Still: One closed archive drawer in brushed bronze, alone in a dark navy space, a single card halfway into the drawer, no markings, no text.

Motion: The card slides the rest of the way in and the drawer shuts. Hold.

## 3. Under silver (section 3, passages)

Still: Three identical translucent strips standing in a row, pale silver edges, dark background, a fine thread of light from the middle strip off to the right, no text.

Motion: The thread brightens and holds. The strips do not move.

## 4. Under the two routes (section 4)

Still: A dark field of small nodes. One path of warm gold links a few nodes. A second path of cool blue drifts through a softer cloud of points. Both arrive at the same small bright card. No text, no icons.

Motion: Light travels along the gold path, then along the blue path, and both rest on the card.

## 5. Under the check (section 4)

Still: A dark gate, a single card held in front of it, a narrow slit of teal light where the card meets the gate, no letters.

Motion: The slit opens and the card passes through. Hold on the empty gate for a beat, then fade.

If a render invents signage, a laptop UI, or a face, drop the clip. The generated frames in `media/` are the explanation.
