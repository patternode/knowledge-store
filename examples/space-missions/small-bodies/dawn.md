# Dawn

Dawn was a NASA mission, managed by the Jet Propulsion Laboratory with the spacecraft built by
Orbital Sciences Corporation, notable for being the first mission to orbit two different
extraterrestrial bodies in sequence: the protoplanet Vesta and the dwarf planet Ceres, both
located in the main asteroid belt between Mars and Jupiter. It launched on September 27, 2007,
from Cape Canaveral aboard a Delta II rocket.

## Ion propulsion

What made visiting two distant bodies practical on a modest budget was Dawn's use of ion
propulsion, in which xenon gas is electrically charged and expelled to produce a very gentle
but highly efficient thrust. The three ion engines gave Dawn far less push than a
conventional chemical rocket, but they could run for months at a time, letting the spacecraft
gradually change its trajectory enough to enter orbit around one body, later leave it, and
enter orbit around another, something no earlier probe had attempted.

## Vesta

Dawn arrived at Vesta in July 2011 and spent about fourteen months in orbit, revealing a
heavily cratered body with a giant impact basin at its south pole and evidence that Vesta had
partly melted and differentiated early in its history, more like a small rocky planet than a
typical asteroid.

## Ceres

After leaving Vesta, Dawn used its ion engines to spiral out to Ceres, the largest object in
the asteroid belt, arriving in March 2015 and becoming the first spacecraft ever to orbit a
dwarf planet. Its cameras and gamma ray and neutron detector found bright deposits of sodium
carbonate salts inside a crater called Occator, evidence of relatively recent activity from
salty liquid, called brine, reaching the surface from within Ceres, hinting at a hidden layer
of liquid or slushy material.

## End of mission

Dawn ran out of the hydrazine fuel needed to keep its antenna pointed at Earth in November
2018, at which point NASA declared the mission complete, leaving the spacecraft in a stable
orbit around Ceres, chosen specifically to avoid any risk of an uncontrolled impact that could
contaminate the dwarf planet for decades to come.
